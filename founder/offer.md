## The stack
**Name: HomeWalk Pre-Construction Walkthrough** ("Let your client walk the house before you pour the footings.")
- **Core**: a branded browser walkthrough + photoreal stills of the home, built to the plan set, on the client's phone in about one business day after complete plans.
- **Bonus 1, zero-effort intake**: we get the plans from your architect; you get a link by text.
- **Bonus 2, revisions included**: 2 rounds until plans are final.
- **Bonus 3, client-ready line item**: suggested wording so it goes on the pre-construction line.
- **Guarantee, Plan-Match**: if anything doesn't match the drawings, we fix it free before you pay. Cost: rework hours only, no refunds paid out because payment happens after approval.
- **Urgency**: none invented. Real constraint only: one founder's capacity per week (say it only once it's true).
- **Prices**: Plan Library $450/plan (10+); Custom $1,500/home; Premium with video $2,500/home.

## Re-test result (20 buyers, seed 926996, pitch v2)
- **Before (pitch v1, 100 buyers): 11% buy. After (pitch v2, 20 buyers): 15% buy (3 of 20).** Still under the 25% bar.
- Moved: plan-library leads 40% (2/5, thin), bulk/website buyers 1 of 2. Lost-renderer builders still 2 of 2.
- Did not move: architect/in-house-covers-it 0/8, small custom builders 0/10, cost-plus 0/3, too busy 0/3.
- Top pass reason shifted from "my architect does it" (habit) to "nobody has asked for it" (need, 10 of 17). The offer answered effort, price and trust; it cannot create demand where clients aren't asking.
- Conclusion: the problem is the market as defined (all Utah custom builders), not mainly the offer or price.
