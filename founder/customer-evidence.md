## Advice from mentors and builders
- **Pricing:**
  - Mentor (Nov 5): the price may be low; "He would have paid $3,000"; keep Danny as a reference customer (W:3a08a2b5).
  - Danny suggested a template base price with customization sold as add-ons, plus $500 revision rounds (P:c9b4c9a3).
  - Mentor (Oct 26): $4,500 is negligible on a $3M home but about 1% of a $400K home; use a one-time fee for luxury builders and a subscription for high-volume builders (W:3ece8292).
  - Instructor: a retainer credited toward per-house fees; interview GCs about the pain of change orders (W:105bf57b).
- **Positioning:**
  - Kyle: there are savings where the architect charges more for 3D, and when buyers use stock plans (W:a5fea9f4).
  - Ally Hunter: become the "single source of truth" across the whole build; know the incumbent tools; designers may resist; talk to many builders and build for the theme they all share (W:9143b61e).
  - Mentor (Oct 27): add a disclaimer and keep finishes generic (W:be82f924).
- **Go-to-market:**
  - Kyle: get leads from agents and architects; give a gift such as a Traeger to the PM rather than cash (W:a5fea9f4).
  - Alex: target architecture firms and interior designers (W:5bacac10).
  - Speaker 3 (Oct 27): pay affiliates by the 15th and "err on the generous side" (W:afbeb4aa).
  - GTM workshop: send a 3-email sequence, then call the top 25%; load the prospect's own floor plan before a demo (W:5aa555b6).
  - Astro (Nov 9): qualify for unbuilt plans first and lead with the build-first offer (C:astro).
