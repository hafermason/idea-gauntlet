# 3D Walkthroughs (working name)

- What it is: Turns a luxury home builder's 2D site plans / floor plans into a 3D walkthrough the builder can include in the pricing package for a home that has not been built yet.
- Who it is for: Luxury custom and semi-custom home builders in Utah.
- What it sells, at what price: Per-home 3D visualization. Basic home: $1,500. Premium: up to $4,000, which includes a video flythrough of the home and high-quality rendered images.
- Where and how: Utah only, in person. The founder builds a free sample walkthrough of one of a builder's plans, then shows it to the builder over lunch in person; reputation and referrals among Utah builders are the growth engine. Models are made with AI tools and Blender.
- Budget and constraints: A home takes the founder a few hours of actual working time and about $10 in direct costs (AI tools/compute). Solo founder doing the modeling. Cash budget, hours per week available and existing builder relationships: not stated yet.

## Additional context from the founder

- The founder's go-to-market is: pick a builder, make a sample walkthrough of one of their plans unprompted, take the builder to lunch, show it in person.
- The walkthrough is meant to be bundled into the builder's pricing for the home-to-be-built (i.e. the builder passes the cost to, or uses it to sell, their own client before construction).
- Deliverables named so far: 3D walkthrough; for premium, a video flythrough and high-quality still images.
