# Offers lens
Score: 6/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Buyers with money who are easy to find.** Luxury custom builders in one state are a short, nameable list, and they sell homes where $1,500-$4,000 is a small line item. The rest of the market test is not proven: is the pain massive, and is the market growing? Both are open.
- **Low effort and fast for the buyer.** The builder hands over plans they already have. Delivery takes a few hours of founder time. On the value equation, time and effort are already in good shape.
- **The free sample is proof.** Showing a finished walkthrough of the builder's own plan before asking for money is the best way to raise perceived likelihood. The buyer sees the result before paying.
- **Margins.** About $10 in direct cost against $1,500-$4,000 leaves room to add bonuses or a guarantee.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **It sells a deliverable, not an outcome.** The builder's dream outcome is a client who signs sooner, picks upgrades or haggles less, not a walkthrough file. Right now nothing shows the walkthrough moves any of those. Without that, the builder sees a nice extra, and nice extras get cut first.
2. **The market may be too small or too slow.** Revenue is one-off and per home, sold one lunch at a time by one person. How many builders fit the target, and how many homes each starts a year, decides whether this is a business or a side income. Both numbers are missing.
3. **Commodity pressure.** The founder can make this cheaply with AI tools and Blender, so anyone else can too, including the builder's own architect or designer. If the only edge is price or convenience, buyers will price-shop and the margin goes away.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- How many Utah builders fit the target, and how many homes does a typical one start or pre-sell each year?
- What do these builders use today to sell an unbuilt home, and what does it cost them?
- Has any builder said the walkthrough would help close a sale? Would they pay before seeing a sample?
- Who pays in the end: the builder, or the home buyer through the builder's pricing? Does the builder add a markup?
- How many hours a week can you deliver, and how many homes a month does that cap you at?
- What guarantee could you offer, for example delivery in a set number of days or you do not get paid?
- Could you charge $8,000 if the stack included sales-ready images, listing video and a version for each upgrade option?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Paid proof.** Within 90 days, at least three builders pay list price for a home after seeing the free sample.
2. **The market numbers.** A count of target builders and their yearly home volume, showing enough repeat demand to reach the founder's income goal.
3. **An outcome-based stack.** Change the offer from "a 3D walkthrough" to a named sales package built around the builder's goal, with a turnaround guarantee. Then add per-builder recurring terms, such as a set price for every home they list, so revenue does not reset with each home.
