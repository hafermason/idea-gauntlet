# Monopoly lens
Score: 4 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **A small market that can be owned.** Luxury custom and semi-custom builders in Utah, sold in person, is the kind of narrow, reachable market this lens likes. One person can plausibly become *the* walkthrough provider in that circle.
- **Distribution is designed in, not bolted on.** An unprompted free sample built on the builder's own plan, shown over lunch, is a real way to reach buyers. Referrals among a tight group of builders could keep acquisition cost low.
- **Unit economics leave room.** About $10 in direct cost against $1,500-$4,000 a home, so margin is not the problem. Hours are the constraint.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No secret and no 10x technology.** The tools are AI plus Blender, which anyone can use. Architectural visualization is an established service, and builders can already buy renders from studios, freelancers or plan-drawing firms. This is an incremental improvement in cost and speed, not a breakthrough. By month six a copycat with the same tools can match the output and undercut the price. Who else already sells to these builders is an open question.
2. **Durability rests on one person's time and relationships.** A few hours a home with a solo modeler means no economies of scale. The only moat in sight is brand and reputation inside the Utah builder circle, which is real but thin and goes away if the founder steps back. In ten years, falling AI costs may let builders or their architects do this in-house.
3. **The small market may be too small to dominate profitably, with no expansion path shown.** How many luxury builders there are in Utah, how many homes each sells a year, and what share would pay per home are all open questions. Utah-only and in-person caps the ceiling unless there is a route to the next market.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Who else sells 3D walkthroughs or renders to Utah luxury builders today, and at what price?
- How many target builders are there in Utah, and how many homes a year would they each run through this?
- What do you know about these builders or this workflow that the studios and freelancers do not? That is your secret.
- What stops a Blender freelancer with the same AI tools from copying you in six months?
- What compounds as you go? A library of builder plans and finishes, an integration with how builders price and sell, exclusivity?
- How many lunches does it take to land one paying builder, and what does each cost in hours?
- After Utah, what is the next market, and does the model work there without in-person lunches?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Name the competitors within reach of these builders, with their prices, and give one clear reason this is 10x better on something builders care about, not just cheaper.
2. Size the beachhead: number of target builders and homes a year, showing that a big share of it is worth owning.
3. Name the moat to be built (for example, exclusive builder relationships, a reusable asset library, or embedding in the builder's sales and pricing process) and show early proof of it with the first paying builders.
4. Get at least a few builders paying repeatedly through the lunch-and-sample channel, with the acquisition cost per builder recorded.
