# Pricing: HomeWalk

Inputs: `founder/pricing-curve.md` (100 simulated buyers), `founder/competitors.md`, real quotes in `founder/customer-evidence.md`. The CFO stage has not run yet, so margins below use only the stated ~$10 direct cost per home; founder hours per home are not yet measured (an open input for the CFO).

## What buyers said (simulated, Van Westendorp)
- Acceptable range **$398 – $1,999**; least resistance (OPP) ~$406; indifference (IPP) ~$897.
- By buyer type (median "bargain" / "getting expensive" / "too expensive"):
  - Lost their renderer: $900 / $2,200 / $3,500 (the only group that bought)
  - Bulk/website library buyers: $400 / $1,100 / $2,000
  - Architect already covers it: $550 / $1,500 / $2,500
- $1,500 sits inside the range. **$4,000 is above the point where most say "too expensive" (~$2,000)**, and above every group's median except lost-renderer builders.

## What the market charges (from competitors.md and real calls)
- AI floor-plan tools ~$20–140; ArchiCGI ~$1,350 for 5 stills, ~$3,150 with a 60-sec video; Utah plan designers bundling renders + video $4,000+; architects' 3D upcharge $3,000–5,000 (Valor, Nov 2) and ~$4,500–5,000 (Kyle Wilson, Nov 2); Walk Your Plans ~$1,000/hr; Idaho operator $4,500/home.
- Real price paid: $1,500/home (Blackbird, Nov 4). Quoted: ~$1,250/plan for 45 plans (Sierra), ~$1,000/plan (Beacon).

## The price
1. **Custom home walkthrough: $1,500** (unchanged). Inside the range, the price a real builder paid, and under the architect upcharge it would replace. Now **includes 2 revision rounds until plans are final**; extra rounds $250 (was $500 every round, a top objection).
2. **Premium (adds video flythrough + extra hero stills): $2,500** (down from $4,000). Above $2,000 most panel buyers balk; $2,500 is still under ArchiCGI's ~$3,150 for a similar bundle and within lost-renderer builders' range.
3. **Plan Library (10+ plans, for builder websites): $450 per plan**, revisions on standard options included. Near the panel's least-resistance price and bulk buyers' "bargain" point; well under the $1,000–1,250 quoted to Beacon/Sierra, which got no decision. 45 plans ≈ $20,250.

Margin check: at ~$10 direct cost every tier clears its direct cost by 97%+. The real constraint is hours per plan, which the CFO must measure; at $450/plan the library only works if a plan takes a few hours, including revisions.

## Ladder
Plan Library ($450/plan, volume) → Custom Walkthrough ($1,500/home) → Premium ($2,500/home). Step-up reasons: one specific client's home and finishes (custom); marketing video for a listing or Parade entry (premium).

## Opening offer
Unchanged mechanism, already proven once: build one of their plans first, no deposit, pay only if they like it. For library buyers: the first plan is built before they commit to the rest. No fake "was" prices.

## What to test with real builders
- Library: $450 vs $650 per plan, offered to Sierra (45 plans) and Beacon (57 plans), the two open bulk leads.
- Custom: $1,500 with 2 revisions included vs $1,750 with unlimited revisions until plans lock, on the next 5 custom-builder lunches.

## Top price objections, verbatim (for marketing)
- "My architect already does 3D for us, and I bill architecture and pre-construction cost-plus, so a new $1,500 line only goes in if the homeowner agrees to pay it." (P008)
- "I'm not putting $1,500 a house on a library of 20-100 plans." (P005)
- "$500 a round on top of $1,500 adds up." (P069)
