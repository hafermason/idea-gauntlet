# Buyer panel: HomeWalk (hellohomewalk.com): turns a builder's 2D house plans into a browser-based 3D walkthrough and photoreal stills before the home is built

**11 buy · 89 pass** (11% buy) out of 100 simulated buyers. Seed 926996, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- Seeing my own plan done over lunch and it matching to the inch, plus a clear written rule that every walkthrough is labeled as a rendering and revisions are free when the plans change.
- Seeing the walkthrough of one of my own plans, built free, that a client or the boss would actually look at, plus a bulk per-plan price well under $1,500 for putting many plans on the site.
- A flat price for my whole plan library, or proof that it closes spec home sales faster and the buyer pays for it, not me.
- A client or architect I work with asking for it by name, or a builder I know showing me it caught a layout change that saved them real money on a job.
- If it cost a few hundred dollars per plan, covered my whole plan library with a model reused on every lot, and I could see it match one of my plans exactly at the free lunch demo, I'd try it for my model-home sales.
- Seeing that buyers sign faster or pay more for upgrades when they walk the home first, ideally with a flat price for my whole plan library instead of per-home pricing.
- If it turned out clients actually sign faster and stop making expensive change orders after framing, and I saw it on my own plan over lunch, I might try it on one job where the homeowner agrees to the line item.
- Nothing about the offer itself. It would take you handling everything with zero work from me, like pulling a plan off our shared drive and sending me a link I can open on my phone from the truck.
- If my architect's 3D didn't cover finishes and lighting and a client of mine was stalling on selections, and you could just take the plans from the architect and send me the link with zero effort from me.
- Proof that it sells homes faster in a Parade of Homes setting, and a price low enough per plan (a few hundred dollars) that I could just run my whole library through it without anyone on my team having to manage it.
- A client of mine asking for it, or seeing it actually cut change orders on a job I'm already running, without me having to do any work to set it up.
- A homeowner telling me they'd happily pay for it, or evidence that it closes sales faster or cuts change orders enough to pay for itself.

Buyers say they would buy **1.1 times** in the first month on average.
